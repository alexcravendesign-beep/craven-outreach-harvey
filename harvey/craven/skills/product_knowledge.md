## Bottle coolers and back-bar (pubs, bars, cafes with a drinks fridge)
- What it is: the glass-door fridge behind the bar or on the counter that holds bottles and cans at serving temperature and shows them off.
- Models: TEFCOLD BC85 glass-door merchandiser, about £324 (undercounter, single door) [shop 21 Oct 2026]; TEFCOLD BC145 undercounter chiller, about £323 [shop]; TEFCOLD DB202S / DB302S back-bar coolers, about £463 / £577 [shop]; TEFCOLD UR200SG glass-door merchandiser, about £385 [shop].
- Running cost: BC85 uses 387 kWh a year on its datasheet, about £97 a year at 25p [Tefcold datasheet; Craven convention]. Older bar fridges commonly use double that, but do not state a figure for the venue's current unit.
- Why Tefcold: R290 refrigerant, low noise (45 dB(A) on the BC85 datasheet), spares and service available locally.
- Honest objection answers: "the old one still works" → it does until a Friday night in July; the question is what it costs to run and whether it holds temperature when the bar is full. "Too dear" → a single-door glass unit is a few hundred pounds, not thousands.
