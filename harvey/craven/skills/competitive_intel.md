# Competitive intel — who a Swansea venue buys from instead

No knocking. The writer never names a competitor in an email. This is for understanding the objection behind "we have a supplier".

- **Nisbets** — the catalogue everyone knows; wide range, next-day delivery, no local engineer. Our difference: the kit is chosen by someone who fits and services it in South Wales, and the same people come back when it breaks.
- **Adexa / FFD / Corr Chilled** — online-only refrigeration specialists, keen prices, kerbside delivery. Our difference: showroom in Swansea you can walk into, installation and after-sales from the same firm, Tefcold-family range stocked rather than drop-shipped.
- **Local refrigeration engineers (one-man firms)** — trusted, often cheap on labour, limited stock and no display range. Our difference: three engineers plus a stocked showroom; we are the local firm, with range.
- **Brewery or landlord supply (tied pubs)** — the brewery dictates bar fridges. Our difference: none to force; ask whether they are free to choose before pitching anything.

Objection truths the human can use (not the writer): kerbside delivery and restocking fees are the industry's standard terms and we share them; the honest advantage is proximity, range and service, not price.
